# Bill of Materials (BOM)
## 60% Wooden Keyboard Case

### Wood Stock Requirements

| Item | Specification | Quantity | Notes |
|------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Top Frame Stock | 295mm × 105mm × 6mm (finished to 5mm) | 1 piece | Hardwood: Walnut, Maple, or Cherry |
| Bottom Tray Stock | 295mm × 105mm × 20mm (finished to 15mm) | 1 piece | Same species as top frame |

**Recommended Wood Species:**
- **Walnut**: Dark, premium appearance, moderate hardness (Janka ~1010)
- **Maple**: Light color, very hard (Janka ~1450), excellent stability
- **Cherry**: Medium tone, ages beautifully (Janka ~950)

**Grain Orientation**: Length-wise (along 295mm dimension) for maximum strength

**Stock Preparation Notes:**
- Surface plane both sides to ensure parallel faces within 0.05mm
- Inspect for defects, knots, or cracks before machining
- Allow stock to acclimate to shop environment for 24-48 hours

### Hardware Components

| Item | Specification | Quantity | Purpose | Supplier/Part Number |
|------|--------------|----------|---------|---------------------|
| Brass Inserts | M3 × 5.7mm OD × 4mm length | 6 | Top frame threaded inserts | McMaster-Carr: 94180A331 |
| M2 Screws | M2 × 6mm, Pan Head, Phillips | 6 | PCB mounting to standoffs | McMaster-Carr: 92005A050 |
| M3 Screws | M3 × 12mm, Flat Head, Phillips | 6 | Case assembly (bottom to top) | McMaster-Carr: 92010A120 |
| Rubber Feet | 8mm diameter, adhesive backed, 2mm height | 4 | Anti-slip bottom feet | 3M Bumpon SJ5302 or equivalent |

**Hardware Notes:**
- Brass inserts: Press-fit installation, use arbor press for best results
- M2 screws: Do not over-tighten (max 0.4 Nm torque)
- M3 screws: Countersunk head sits flush with bottom surface
- Rubber feet: Clean surface with isopropyl alcohol before application

**Finishing Process Notes:**
- Sand progressively through all grits (120 → 220 → 320)
- Remove all dust with tack cloth before applying finish
- Apply finish in thin, even coats
- Allow proper drying time between coats (follow manufacturer instructions)
- Light sand with 320 grit between coats for best adhesion

### Storage and Shelf Life

| Item | Storage Conditions | Shelf Life |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| Wood Stock | Climate controlled, flat storage | Indefinite if kept dry |
| Brass Inserts | Dry location | Indefinite |
| Screws | Dry location | Indefinite |
| Rubber Feet | Room temperature, sealed | 2-3 years |
| Sandpaper | Dry location | Indefinite |
| Oil Finishes | Sealed container, cool location | 1-2 years opened |
| Polyurethane | Sealed container, cool location | 2-3 years opened |

**Safety Notes:**
- Oil-soaked rags are fire hazards - lay flat to dry or store in water-filled metal container
- Dispose of finishing materials according to local regulations
- Use adequate ventilation when applying finishes
- Wear appropriate PPE (dust mask, gloves, safety glasses)
