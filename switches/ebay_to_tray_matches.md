# eBay Lot → Switch Tray Matches (IMG_3220.JPG)

Cross-reference of the 29 labeled switches from the eBay listing photos
(`fromebay1.PNG`, `fromebay2.webp`) against the physical switch tester tray in
`IMG_3220.JPG` (4 rows × 15 columns, top-left = `0,0`).

Matches are based on visual identification (housing color, stem color, box
vs. MX style, visible branding). Confidence key:

- **High** — distinctive appearance, unambiguous match
- **Med** — appearance matches but similar-looking switches exist in the tray
- **Low** — plausible but needs hands-on verification
