STANDOFF PLACEMENT (5-6 per half):
• Follow your PCB's mounting hole pattern exactly
• Use M3 x 8-12mm standoffs 
• Countersink screws from bottom for flush mount
• PCB sits on top of standoffs

KEY ADVANTAGES OF TRAY MOUNT FOR LILY58:
✓ Simple construction - just drill holes
✓ Forgiving tolerances for irregular shape
✓ Easy access to Pro Micro and connections  
✓ No complex plate recess routing needed
✓ Strong and reliable mounting system

REQUIRED CUTOUTS PER HALF:
• TRRS Jack: 7mm diameter hole (connects halves)
• Pro Micro: 20mm x 35mm rectangle (access/removal)  
• USB-C: 9mm x 3.5mm slot (power/data)

CONSTRUCTION STEPS:
1. Create template from plate files
2. Route/cut main cavity 
3. Drill standoff holes (M3)
4. Cut access holes (TRRS, Pro Micro, USB)
5. Test fit all components
6. Final assembly with standoffs
