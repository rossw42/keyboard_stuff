✅ Key Advantages of Tray Mount

  • 🔨 Simple Construction: Just drill holes and screw in standoffs
  • 📐 Forgiving Tolerances: Perfect for irregular Lily58 shape
  • 🔧 Easy Component Access: Pro Micro and connections accessible
  • ⚡ No Complex Routing: No plate recess cutting required
  • 💪 Strong & Reliable: Solid mounting system
  • 🪵 Wood-Friendly: Works great with natural wood properties

🛠️ Construction Steps

  1. Create Template: Use Lily58 plate files to make cutting template
  2. Route Main Cavity: Hollow out space for PCB and components
  3. Drill Standoff Holes: M3 holes following PCB mounting pattern
  4. Cut Access Holes: TRRS, Pro Micro, and USB cutouts
  5. Test Fit Everything: Ensure all components fit properly
  6. Final Assembly: Install standoffs and build keyboard
