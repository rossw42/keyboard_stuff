# Design Comparison: Current vs Porne

## Current Hypergarlic Design (Simple Tray)

**Case Bottom:**
- Simple tray with walls
- Flat bottom
- M3 screw holes in corners only
- PCB sits directly on bottom
- Height: ~6.5mm

**Plate:**
- 1.5mm thick
- Switch cutouts
- M2.5 mounting holes in corners
- Sits on top of case

**Assembly:** Sandwich mount with screws through corners

## Porne Design (Advanced with Standoffs)

**Case Bottom:**
- Integrated standoffs (~32 cylindrical posts)
- Plate lip/ledge around perimeter
- Standoffs have screw holes
- More complex geometry
- Better support for plate

**Plate:**
- Mounts to standoffs
- Sits on lip for stability
- Switch cutouts
- More mounting points

**Assembly:** Plate screws into standoffs, sits on lip

## To Upgrade Hypergarlic to Porne-Style

Would need to add:

1. **Standoffs** - Cylindrical posts at strategic locations (near switches)
2. **Plate Lip** - Raised edge around perimeter for plate to rest on
3. **Screw Bosses** - Threaded inserts or holes in standoffs
4. **Proper Clearances** - Space for PCB, components, and plate

This is significantly more complex than current design.
