## Assembly Notes

### Critical Information
- **Zener diodes:** Keep 3.6V Zener diodes SEPARATE from 1N4148 diodes - they look similar!
- **Stabilizers:** Only screw-in stabilizers are supported (plate mount will NOT work)
- **USB Port:** Use flux paste and drag soldering technique for the small pins
- **Testing:** Highly recommended to test PCB functionality before soldering switches

### Layout Decisions

**Bottom Row Stabilizer:**
- [ ] 6.25u spacebar (standard)
- [ ] 7u spacebar (alternative)

**Layout Type:**
- [ ] ANSI (requires 3x 2u stabilizers)
- [ ] ISO (requires 2x 2u stabilizers)

**Switch Count:**
- [ ] 67 switches (standard layouts)
- [ ] 68 switches (with extra key)

## Build Order (From Guide)

1. ☐ **Step 1:** Solder 68x 1N4148 diodes (polarized - black bar up)
2. ☐ **Step 2:** Solder 2x 3.6V Zener diodes (polarized - black bar up)
3. ☐ **Step 3:** Solder resistors (non-polarized)
4. ☐ **Step 4:** Solder USB Type-C port (use flux!)
5. ☐ **Step 5:** Solder 2x 6mm pushbuttons
6. ☐ **Step 6:** Solder 6-pin header and 500mA fuse
7. ☐ **Step 7:** Solder 3mm LED (polarized) and 4.7uF capacitor (polarized)
8. ☐ **Step 8:** Solder 16MHz crystal
9. ☐ **Step 9:** Solder 2x 22pF capacitors
10. ☐ **Step 10:** Solder 2x 0.1uF capacitors
11. ☐ **Step 11:** Solder 40-pin IC socket, then insert Atmega32A
12. ☐ **Step 12:** Test PCB functionality with key tester
13. ☐ **Step 13:** Install stabilizers
14. ☐ **Step 14:** Install switches with plate
15. ☐ **Step 15:** Install keycaps
16. ☐ **Step 16:** Test completed build
